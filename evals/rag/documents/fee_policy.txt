FEE POLICY

Tuition fees are payable in two instalments. The first instalment is due on 15 July and the second instalment is due on 15 January.

LATE FEE

A late fee of Rs. 100 per day applies after the due date, up to a maximum of Rs. 3,000. Students with fees pending beyond 30 days will not receive their hall ticket.

REFUNDS

Fees are refunded in full if admission is cancelled before the start of classes. After classes begin, 50% of the tuition fee is refunded if the student withdraws within 15 days. No refund is given after 15 days.
