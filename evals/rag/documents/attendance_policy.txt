ATTENDANCE POLICY

Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in every course to be eligible to write the end semester examination. Attendance is calculated separately for theory and laboratory sessions.

CONDONATION

Students whose attendance is between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation on medical grounds. The application must include a medical certificate and be submitted to the Head of Department within seven days of the attendance notice. A condonation fee of Rs. 500 applies.

SHORTAGE

Students below 65% attendance are not eligible for condonation and must repeat the course in the next academic year.
