LIBRARY RULES

Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books for 14 days. Postgraduate students may borrow up to six books for 21 days. A fine of Rs. 5 per day applies to each book returned late. Reference books and journals cannot be taken out of the library.

HOSTEL RULES

Hostel gates close at 9:30 pm on weekdays and at 10:30 pm on weekends. Visitors are allowed in the common room between 4 pm and 7 pm. Cooking appliances are not permitted in hostel rooms.
